# Research standards

Our aim is **reasoned, rights-respecting, testable research**. Research is not automatically an adopted Fusion policy.

## Claims and evidence

- Link to primary sources where practical: legislation, budgets, official datasets, academic studies and original reporting.
- Separate **verified facts**, **estimates**, **assumptions**, **value judgements** and **proposals**.
- Give the publication date, jurisdiction and relevant timeframe for claims that may change.
- Explain how figures were calculated, including units, baselines, price year and uncertainty.
- Present meaningful counterarguments and plausible alternatives. Do not treat lack of evidence as evidence of absence.

## Policy feasibility

For substantial proposals, examine legal authority, responsible institutions, operating and capital costs, funding, implementation stages, dependencies, stakeholders, risks, accessibility, civil liberties and distributional impacts. Avoid presenting gross savings as net fiscal gains or financing changes as new revenue.

## Research status

Every paper should identify its jurisdiction, author/contributors, status, last review date and sources. Suggested statuses: **idea**, **draft**, **under review**, **revised**, **archived**. **Endorsed** should only be used where formal endorsement can be linked and verified.

## AI-assisted work

AI can help discover questions, organise drafts, compare alternatives and red-team assumptions. Authors remain accountable for every published claim. Independently check citations and quotations; do not cite an AI answer as evidence. Disclose substantial AI assistance and note what was independently verified.

## Updates

When material changes, record a short revision note. Date-sensitive research should be periodically revisited. Retain important rejected options and corrections so future contributors understand why decisions changed.

## Review culture

A review should identify the exact claim or assumption at issue, the evidence supporting the objection and, where possible, a path to resolve it. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

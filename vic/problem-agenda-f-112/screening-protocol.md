# Proposed screening protocol — F113-PB01

Version 1.0, 17 October 2026. **Unconfirmed. No records screened under this protocol.** Source: F-112 outcome-led agenda. All added operational criteria below are **proposed**, for human review. This is a bounded comparative evidence brief using PICO plus implementation evidence, not a registered systematic review.

Question: Which feasible responses improve safe, affordable housing retention among low-income renters at risk of tenancy loss, compared with current provision or an alternative, with what harms and unequal effects?

## Proposed eligibility

Population: low-income/financially vulnerable renters or households at credible risk of eviction/tenancy loss. At title/abstract stage accept a plausibly relevant subgroup. Already-homeless populations are a separate contextual stratum unless prevention/retention outcomes can be separated.

Responses: financial, legal, case-support, service-access or housing-access interventions intended to prevent loss of safe affordable housing; include materially different alternatives and unchanged provision. Pure construction-finance or general dwelling-efficiency studies without tenancy-retention outcomes are context/out-of-scope for the effect stratum.

Comparators: current practice, no new intervention, or another eligible response. A comparator is required for causal-effect claims, **not** for descriptive baseline or qualitative implementation evidence. Distinguish effect, baseline and implementation strata.

Outcomes: safe/affordable housing retention, eviction, homelessness/re-entry, safety, wellbeing, unequal access, harms or relevant costs. Retention alone is not evidence of safe affordable retention. Designs: RCTs, quasi-experiments, longitudinal comparative studies for causal evidence; official descriptive data for baselines; transparent qualitative/mixed-methods evaluation for implementation; systematic reviews for synthesis/context with overlap checks.

Jurisdiction: Victoria/Australia first; other comparable high-income jurisdictions if relevant transfer can be assessed at full text. Date: prioritise 2000–2026; older sources flagged for relevance rather than excluded automatically. Language: no exclusion solely by language; translation unavailable remains pending. Published/grey literature eligible; preprints flagged. No paid access or new credentials.

**Full-text-only criteria must not be used to exclude at title/abstract stage:** extractable outcomes for the eligible subgroup; methods sufficient to place the report in an evidence stratum; comparator suitability for a causal claim; transferability details; full-text access. Missing full text is an access exception, not an exclusion.

## Operational definitions and unclear cases

Housing retention counts continued or alternative safe stable accommodation, using the source's definition and follow-up. It does not count reduced arrears, service engagement or improved warmth alone as proof of retention. Low income counts an explicit income threshold, benefit eligibility or documented financial hardship; unclear thresholds advance for clarification. Missing abstracts with plausible population/response in the title advance as UNC. Mixed cohorts advance if relevant subgroup results might be available. Ambiguous geography/design advances for full-text assessment.

INC means all title/abstract-assessable criteria are met; UNC means core population and response are plausible but details missing; both advance. Exclude only on a clear failure and give the **first** failing code below, plus a short reason and record ID. Record text only; do not fill gaps from recall. Never exclude because a study is null, negative, politically awkward or contradicts a proposal.

| Order/code | Criterion | Stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | No research/data/evaluation content: advertisement, opinion-only commentary or unrelated announcement | Title/abstract when clear; otherwise full text |
| E2 | Clearly no eligible population or plausible subgroup | Title/abstract or full text |
| E3 | Clearly unrelated response/concept; no retention/prevention link | Title/abstract or full text |
| E4 | No eligible outcome usable for any declared evidence stratum | Full text only |
| E5 | Methods insufficient to support any declared evidence stratum, after clarification | Full text only |
| E6 | Jurisdiction/context cannot inform Victoria after an explicit transfer assessment | Full text only |

Duplicate detection occurs before screening; link duplicate bibliographic records to a master without losing original identifiers. Companion reports remain linked, not silently discarded. Reviews and protocols are not E1 by default: protocols supply context, not completed effects. No broad catch-all exclusion.

## Review and quality controls (proposed)

Human confirmation covers this protocol, config and synthetic cases. Record human approver/date/hash before pilot. Reviewer A: housing-services subject lens; B: causal-methods/transfer lens; separate calls receive the same frozen text, without each other's decisions. Third reviewer adjudicates advance/exclude conflict. Labels are decision support; shared-model role separation does not establish independent human review. No such calls occurred here.

Humans label the bounded calibration set independently; compare AI exclusions against all human advances, resolve disagreements and confirm adequacy before full screening. Obtain explicit fan-out cost approval if used. Joint exclusions receive all near-miss rechecks plus a seeded random sample of at least 20, or all when fewer; pending rechecks stay pending. Humans check every advanced record and a sample of exclusions before final reporting. Do not compute kappa/PABAK, PRISMA counts or completeness from an unrun protocol.

## Synthetic boundary cases — proposed expectations, not screened studies

1. Rental-arrears RCT in financially vulnerable renters, retention at 12 months: INC, effect stratum.
2. Title-only “Legal advocacy to prevent eviction in Australia”: UNC; population/response plausible, abstract absent.
3. Insulation RCT reports warmth only and no housing retention: E3 for this effect question; retain as housing-quality context in a separate ledger.
4. Advertising a tenancy app with no data, for high-income owners: E1 first, even though E2 also fails.
5. Qualitative evaluation of support barriers among low-income renters, no comparator: INC, implementation stratum; cannot establish causal effect.
6. Comparative homelessness program with mixed cohorts and uncertain at-risk-renter subgroup: UNC; full text may separate outcomes.

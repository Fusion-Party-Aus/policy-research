# Methods, source coverage and limitations

Research date: 17 October 2026 AEDT (16 October UTC). AI author: Policy Researcher. Work type: bounded problem-agenda evidence brief for internal human review. The question, domain frame, ratings and source selection are analyst judgements. No primary human-subject data, sensitive membership/donor data or paid services were accessed.

## Workflows used and consulted

Deep-research **quick** mode guided question specification, purposive source discovery, verification and a concise brief with supporting inventories. Roles were carried out inline in one session; no multi-agent pipeline or independent reviewers were run. Official descriptive measures were graded for their actual measurement purpose rather than treated as causal evidence. The brief is intentionally not a systematic evidence synthesis or a journal manuscript.

Sr-screener **protocol** guidance supplied the proposed eligibility/configuration and boundary cases for the later batch. No formal title/abstract or full-text screening has occurred. Its protocol/pilot/cost/human-verification gates remain unfulfilled. Drafting a protocol is this stage's deliverable, not authorisation to execute it. AI role separation in a future workflow would not create independent human peer review.

Academic-paper **citation-check guidance** was used to reconcile source identifiers, article identity, DOI, dates, claim locators, access states and orphan references. Citation format follows the repository's source-linked policy-brief convention, rather than journal typesetting. A full automated academic-paper compliance pipeline, Semantic Scholar verification of every source, comprehensive Retraction Watch database check and plagiarism service were not run. Primary agency pages establish source identity; the one journal study has PubMed/Europe PMC identity corroboration. Limited retraction/correction web searches found no alert; this is not a guarantee of no correction/retraction.

Academic-paper-reviewer **policy-brief criteria** were used for a single-session methodological self-critique, recorded in `quality-review.md`. This is not a five-seat review, a calibrated review outcome or independent peer review. Academic-pipeline was inspected for scope; the full research-to-manuscript workflow was not invoked because this task is a stage-1 agenda, not manuscript production. No full-pipeline or PRISMA compliance is claimed. Multica-platform instructions govern issue delivery and attachment handling.

## Discovery and inventory boundary

`search-log.csv` records 42 exact discovery/verification query strings reconstructed from the tool calls. Search engine: general web search with explicit primary-domain tokens where practical; sources were opened adaptively. Direct opens, within-document searches and the Europe PMC XML fetch supplement those discovery queries. The log is a query-level record, not a complete export of every returned candidate, ranking position or rejected snippet. Tool-returned result sets were not preserved in full. It therefore cannot produce PRISMA counts or search-recall estimates.

The retained source inventory is **18 unique source records**, with **20 source–problem evidence rows**. These are a purposive selection for operationalising needs, not 18 independently replicated studies or a completed screening population. They include one catalogue-only currency exception and one high-level climate summary not fully appraised. A VAGO service review is reused across three problems; AIHW suicide data reuse the Coroners Court register. Repeated sources or shared underlying data are not independent corroboration.

Baseline sources were prioritised for jurisdiction/measure fit. Reviews, audits and observational data cannot establish an intervention's causal effects. The single full-text RCT is contextual evidence of a state-relevant housing-quality mechanism and its null results; it is outside the proposed tenancy-retention effect question. Its primary temperature result and secondary outcome uncertainty were distinguished. The historical external perpetrator evaluation was read only as a summary and does not supply a causal violence-reduction verdict.

Sources and queries not retained as evidence include media, Reddit, party research, irrelevant search returns, historical mental-health audit snippets that cannot establish current reform effects, and education pages without extracted outcome data. Energy-disconnection figures were discovered but not used because the opened regulator PDF could not be inspected sufficiently; the essentials baseline instead uses the clearly defined Victorian food item. No conclusion of low energy hardship follows. The search did not comprehensively cover chronic disease, gambling, work insecurity, all disability services, water or governance outcomes. Those remain explicit gap-register questions.

## Currency, access and measurement

`sources.csv` distinguishes publication/update dates, reference periods, access states, limitations and source locators. Date-like search-result age labels were not treated as publication dates. The source cut-off is the run date; this does not mean every retained observation is the latest available. The rental page exposed September 2025 figures, Census homelessness reflects 2021, and several ecological indicators have older underlying data. June 2026 family-incident metadata were accessible, but the workbook counts were not extracted; the March 2026 baseline is explicitly older. A later extraction can create a separately versioned ranking; do not overwrite the frozen v1.

VAHI ED-length-of-stay access returned 403 in the browser tool; official VAGO reporting was used as a narrower proxy. BMJ/PMC browser access initially failed or showed a challenge. The full S16 article XML was successfully read via `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11865758/fullTextXML`; PubMed PMID 40010814 corroborates authors/title/26 February 2025 date/DOI `10.1136/bmjopen-2023-082340`. `link-checks.csv` records one bounded network check per listed URL; status 200 alone does not prove substantive access. No anti-bot bypass, paid access or new credentials were used.

AEDC values were checked against the official one-page PDF text and prose. The requested browser screenshot did not yield a visually inspected image in the tool context, and no local PDF rasteriser was installed. A visual chart check remains a review limitation; no new software was installed. Official raw data and underlying survey confidence intervals would strengthen validation.

Population coverage, trend comparability and missing denominators are explicit in `measurement-plan.csv`. Food measures concern different units and definitions; new rents are not all rents; allocated waiting times exclude those still waiting; incident counts are not unique victims; suicide surveillance is provisional; climate anomalies do not measure harm attribution; custody rates of different standardisation must not be divided. Distributional consequences not quantified in the sources are hypotheses, not invented measured gaps. No combined total of affected people or monetary harm is reported.

## Ranking and corpus separation

The six core files plus frozen manifest were written and staged before reading the corpus contents. Git commit failed because no author identity is configured; no identity was changed. `git write-tree` produced the immutable tree in `freeze-receipt.json` before the corpus read. SHA-256 entries in `ranking-frozen.json` allow verification of the core bytes; this is local sequencing evidence, not third-party preregistration or proof of researcher blinding.

Issue history/onboarding and an incidental party search snippet disclosed some context; this was not a blinded experiment. Candidate discovery and scoring were performed without opening the export contents or F-107 queue. Later explanatory rationales and measurement plans do not change the frozen ratings. Alternative weights demonstrate preference sensitivity but cannot validate the original ordinal judgements or rule out omitted domains.

Post-freeze corpus mapping reuses the revised F-107 attachment and unchanged original snapshots, not a new import. All 41 policy and 25 resource IDs/pointers/hashes reconcile in `corpus-coverage.csv`; scope is the public export, not full Sanity. Mapping is limited to the declared inspected fields and metadata, with full underlying external documents unappraised. “No dedicated response identified” is search-bounded, not an assertion that Fusion has no such position anywhere. New political analysis was not pushed to the public repository; no existing proposal wording was changed.

Mika coordinates progression after acceptance. No later stage, sub-agent, schedule, repeated monitoring, policy adoption, public publication or production change was initiated.

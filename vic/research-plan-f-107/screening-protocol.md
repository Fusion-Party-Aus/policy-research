# Proposed B1 screening protocol

> **Research — not adopted Fusion policy**

**Version:** 0.1, 17 October 2026 AEDT. **Confirmation:** NOT CONFIRMED. **Status:** planning only; no records screened. **Source:** F-107 stage-2 work-plan request. All eligibility details below are analyst proposals for human review, not inferred author decisions.

Review form: bounded policy evidence pass, not a systematic review. Framework: PCC for access baselines (population, useful transport access, Melton/metropolitan context), PICO for comparative service effects. Question: which bus/transport-access options improve meaningful access for underserved Melton residents, at what cost and with what distributional consequences?

## Eligibility and operational definitions [proposed]

**Core criteria:** (1) people/communities using, needing or planning local passenger transport; (2) useful access or a service, fare, walking/interchange or demand-responsive mechanism relevant to B1. Useful access includes destinations reachable, time, reliability, span, safe accessible walking, refusal/booking constraints or household travel cost. Stop proximity or ridership alone can inform context but does not establish welfare improvement.

Include primary studies/evaluations, systematic or other transparent reviews, official datasets/audits, legal instruments, public contract/service material and documented demand/operational models. Keep legal/baseline/context evidence distinct from causal-effect evidence. International urban/peri-urban comparators are eligible with transferability flags. Rural studies can advance as context if a plausible relevant mechanism is present; do not silently generalise their costs. Political/commercial material is retained for attribution/context and original claims; it cannot independently corroborate its own proposal.

**Title/abstract assessable:** topical core criteria, clearly ineligible publication purpose, clearly outside the planned date range. Proposed date limits: 2000 onward for effects, preferred 2021 onward for a current baseline. Older foundational studies/standards can advance as explicit exceptions; never exclude an updated legal instrument because its Act title has an older year. Language is a flag only; non-English studies advance if relevant and translation is recorded pending. Missing abstracts are not automatic exclusion.

**Full-text only, never grounds for title/abstract exclusion:** actual counterfactual quality, exact outcome definitions, usable quantitative estimates, model/cost assumptions, sample completeness, subgroup reporting, Victoria transferability and claims of operational feasibility. Absent/unobtainable full text is `awaiting access`, not evidence of ineligibility or a null effect. Poor methods can be included with reduced claim fitness; do not remove inconvenient findings on an undisclosed quality threshold.

## Decisions and ordered exclusion codes [proposed]

`include`/INC: the title/abstract supports the topical core and permitted purpose. `unclear`/UNC: core plausibly met but context, design or details are missing; include and unclear both advance. Exclude only on a definite failure, choosing the first code in this order:

1. **E1 purpose:** no usable evidence/context/primary policy text (e.g. pure commercial advertisement with no methods/data or substantive service information). Reviews and official reports are eligible, not excluded as publication types.
2. **E2 transport/population:** no local passenger-transport/access relevance (e.g. freight-only scheduling).
3. **E3 mechanism/outcome:** no access, service design, fares, demand-response, walking/interchange, costs, implementation or relevant legal content.
4. **E4 timeframe:** clearly before 2000 and no foundational/historical/current-law exception applies.

A record can fail more than one criterion but receives one first-failure code. A title-only “community bus access evaluation” is UNC; “freight depot scheduling” is E2. An official current timetable is INC as baseline context, not causal evidence. An intervention study with no statistically significant benefit is eligible.

## Confirmation, pilot and QC

Confirm both this file and the config with the human review team before any screening. No confirmed seed studies have been supplied; official discovery documents are not gold-labelled eligible studies. If a dual-AI screening run is later requested, first agree synthetic boundary cases, a small human-labelled pilot and a cost estimate. Use fresh isolated reviewer A/B contexts plus an adjudicator for advance/exclude conflicts; different model roles do not establish independent errors or human peer review. Exact runtime model/provider/context IDs must be logged, with existing runtime defaults unless a human instructs otherwise.

No missing decision becomes exclude. Recheck every near-miss joint exclusion and a reproducible random sample (20 minimum, all when fewer). Humans verify advanced records and an exclusion sample before final selection counts. No kappa, PABAK or PRISMA counts exist for this planning stage. Full-text exclusion requires a source location and reason; unresolved access remains pending. For a small later single-reviewer triage, disclose that method explicitly rather than claiming a dual review.

## Selection and screening boundary

This is single-reviewer evidence triage following the accepted topical rules, not a systematic-review selection process. Eligible evidence/context concerns passenger access, services, fares, walking/interchanges, costs, law or implementation relevant to the four mechanisms. Legal/baseline records are distinct from causal-effect studies. Null/adverse findings remain eligible; missing abstracts/full texts are access limitations rather than exclusions. Overseas and rural sources are retained with transferability flags. Political and commercial sources establish their own statements only.

[Candidate-ledger.csv](candidate-ledger.csv) preserves every returned identifier, query group, returned rank and URL. Exact-URL duplicates link to the first occurrence; distinct URLs are **not** silently merged as the same study. Known DOI/repository/publisher aliases are linked to the source family with a separate relation. There are no formal E1–E4 exclusion decisions: candidates not used remain `not appraised` with follow-up status. Raw search snippets and restricted full texts are not republished. Search engine native exports were unavailable; the retained metadata are a capture of the exposed results, not the provider's full index.

Nineteen external document/dataset families received claim-specific appraisal, including limited-text previews and a metadata/contents-only legal record. Four policy records and three internal briefs were also inspected: 26 families in total, below the 40-document ceiling even when framing is counted. Companion HTML/PDF representations and appendices are parts of the same named document, not independent studies. Full read scopes and all access limitations appear in [sources](sources.md). Machine counts are inventory counts; final review selection/acceptance remains human.

## Citation navigation and verification

Backward reference inspection followed VAGO's methodology appendix, IV's WSP modelling reference, WSP's source/validation discussion, the Dutch study's fixed/DRT comparison references, and UWE's evaluation references. These leads are not automatically included findings. Forward coverage is narrower than planned: S2 DOI lookups returned citation metadata for the Dutch, Richmond and Palma papers; the Dutch publisher also exposes citing articles. Their 217 returned citation-metadata occurrences are preserved separately in [forward-citation-leads.csv](forward-citation-leads.csv); they remain unappraised discovery leads. No complete forward pass for the five initially prioritised official/report sources was achieved. This is a material coverage shortfall, not evidence of saturation.

All cited external families have source URLs and visible identity checks. Four journal titles/years were matched against Crossref and S2 DOI records. Three Elsevier DOI opens failed through the web tool, while publisher-indexed previews and Crossref metadata confirmed identity. The Dutch DOI resolved to accessible full publisher HTML; its institutional PDF returned 403. No full-text access was inferred from an “open access” badge. Crossref update metadata were inspected but a complete Retraction Watch/Crossmark/registration search was not performed; the report makes no “not retracted” certification. No registration was assumed for observational case studies.

Claim fitness is assessed separately from institutional authority. Statistical/causal claims require an appropriate counterfactual; legal facts require the current instrument; modelled effects require assumptions and sensitivity. Partial-text papers supply leads/cautions, not extracted effect sizes or invented methodological weaknesses. Monetary estimates are not transferred between currencies or years. Correlated documents (IV/WSP; DfT/UWE) are kept as shared evidence families.

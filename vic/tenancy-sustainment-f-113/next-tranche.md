## 1. Primary Recommendation: Tranche 2 (F114-PB02) — Family Violence and Repeat Harm

- **Problem ID:** VP-002 (Ranked #2 in Victorian Problem Agenda; weighted score 88.0).
- **Baseline Measure:** 102,674 recorded family incidents in Victoria (year to March 2026), with persistent repeat callout rates and high displacement of women and children ([S06](sources.md#s06)).
- **Nested Research Outcome:** Reducing repeat domestic and family violence, improving survivor-defined physical and financial safety, and eliminating system-induced secondary trauma.

### Proposed Scope and Core Questions
1. **Q01 (Baseline & Trajectories):** What proportion of victim-survivors experience recurrent police-recorded incidents within 12 and 24 months of an initial Family Violence Intervention Order (FVIO), and how do repeat rates vary by children's presence, Aboriginal status, rurality, and weapon involvement?
2. **Q02 (Comparative Intervention Effectiveness):** Relative to standard court-mandated men's behavior change programs (MBCPs) or civil protection orders alone, which interventions measurably reduce repeat physical and coercive violence over 12–24 months? (Evaluate: specialist domestic violence courts, electronic monitoring of high-risk perpetrators, integrated multi-agency risk assessment frameworks [MARAM/The Orange Door], and dedicated survivor economic/housing guarantees).
3. **Q03 (Systemic Harms and Net-Widening):** Does increased police enforcement or proactive charging result in misidentification of victim-survivors as primary aggressors (particularly First Nations women)? Does civil intervention order breach prosecution increase criminal justice entanglement without reducing underlying danger?
4. **Q04 (Feasibility, Powers, and Multi-Jurisdictional Boundaries):** What state powers under the *Family Violence Protection Act 2008 (Vic)*, Victoria Police operational guidelines, and Magistrates' Court operations constrain delivery? How do state protection regimes interact with Commonwealth Family Law Court parenting orders?

### Proposed Finite Batch Caps
- Maximum **12 research hours**.
- **80 unique candidate records**.
- **30 deeply appraised documents**.
- At most eight atomic response-effect assessments.

## 2. Mandatory Concurrent Sentinel Task: VP-012 Baseline Refresh

- **Problem ID:** VP-012 (Child safety and culturally appropriate care in statutory systems).
- **Agenda Status:** Explicit, unranked urgent baseline sentinel gap (preserved from F-112).
- **Task Requirement:** Prior to executing Stage 3 prioritization or multi-problem policy synthesis (F-111), researchers must refresh the statutory baseline metrics for VP-012 using the latest accessible data:
  1. *Commission for Children and Young People (CCYP) Annual Report 2024–25:* Out-of-home care (OOHC) substantiated harm investigations, youth justice oversight, and child death inquiries.
  2. *DFFH Child Protection Annual Reporting:* Total children in out-of-home care in Victoria; rate of Aboriginal children in statutory care compared to non-Aboriginal children; adherence to the Aboriginal Child Placement Principle.
  3. *Transition to Independence Denominators:* Proportion of young people exiting statutory OOHC at age 18 who enter homelessness, justice systems, or unsupported accommodation within 12 months.
